Return Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Return Eligibility

At Nilco Bags, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. You may return eligible items within 7 days of delivery under the following conditions:

  • The item must be unused, unworn, and in original condition
  • All original tags and packaging must be intact
  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation) must be provided
  • The return request must be initiated within 7 days of delivery

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable"
  • Personalized or customized items
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse or negligence
  • Items without original packaging or tags

Return Process

To initiate a return, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer service team via WhatsApp or email
  2. Provide your order number and reason for return
  3. Our team will review your request and provide return authorization
  4. Pack the item securely in original packaging
  5. Ship the item to the address provided by our team

Refund Policy

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

If approved, your refund will be processed as follows:

  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method
  • Refund processing time: 5-7 business days
  • You will receive a confirmation email once the refund is processed

Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer, unless the return is due to:

  • Defective or damaged product received
  • Wrong item shipped
  • Manufacturing defect

In these cases, Nilco Bags will cover the return shipping costs and provide a full refund or replacement.

Exchanges

We accept exchanges for items of the same value or higher. If you choose a higher-priced item, you will be required to pay the difference. Exchanges are subject to availability.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We will:

  • Provide a full refund or replacement at no additional cost
  • Cover return shipping costs
  • Require photos of the damaged item for verification

Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order within 24 hours of placing it, provided the order has not been shipped.

  • Cancellations within 24 hours: Full refund
  • Cancellations after 24 hours: Subject to review and approval
  • Orders already shipped: Cannot be canceled, follow return process instead

Contact Us

For any questions about our return policy or to initiate a return, please contact us:

  • WhatsApp: +91 78713 31144, +91 78713 31155
  • Email: support@nilcobags.com
  • Through our Contact page

Policy Changes

Nilco Bags reserves the right to modify this return policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
